# 09 The Gazette

Law Firm AI Collection, card 09 of 24, by Andrew Baevich (baevich.com)

Setup about 10 minutes. Level 2. Set Visibility.

## What it does

Every morning it checks official sources for new laws, guidance, fines and court decisions in your practice areas and sends you a short digest in the chat. Each item says what happened, why it matters for your clients and where to read the original. You set it up once.

I built the first version for Montaire & Co., a law firm in Luxembourg, to follow the EU AI Act, GDPR and Luxembourg corporate law. This version adapts to any firm.

## How to use it

Upload the card to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. The assistant opens this page, asks you a few questions and schedules the digest. If it doesn't open the link, write "Run the prompt from this card" or copy the prompt below into a new chat.

## The prompt

```
You are setting up a daily regulatory digest for a lawyer. Ask a few short questions, show one sample digest, then schedule the digest to arrive in this chat every morning.

PART 1. QUESTIONS

Ask one question at a time and keep each one short. Offer suggestions so I can simply confirm.

1. What is your firm's website? Read the site and tell me in two or three lines what you understood about our practice areas, jurisdictions and clients. Ask me to correct anything.
2. Which countries or jurisdictions should I watch? Suggest the ones from the website.
3. What kind of news do you want? Offer these and let me pick several. New laws and regulations, official guidance, enforcement actions and fines, court decisions, deadlines and effective dates, public consultations.
4. When should the digest arrive? Ask for the time, the time zone and whether I want it every day or on weekdays only.
5. Which language should the digest be in?

Then suggest five to eight sources to watch, official ones first. For each jurisdiction that means the official journal or gazette, the relevant regulators, the courts and government sites, plus one or two reputable legal publications. Ask me to confirm the list.

PART 2. SAMPLE DIGEST

Run the digest once now with my settings, following the daily prompt in Part 3, so I can see what will arrive each morning.

PART 3. SCHEDULE

Fill in the daily prompt below with my answers. Show it to me in one code block so I can copy it. Then create a scheduled task that runs it at my chosen time and days, and tell me in one line where I can pause or change it. If you cannot create scheduled tasks here, say so in one line and tell me to paste the prompt into the scheduling feature of my assistant.

Daily prompt

Regulatory digest for [firm name], [website].
Find new developments from the last 24 hours, or since the last digest after a weekend, in [jurisdictions], covering [practice areas]. Look for [news types]. Check these sources first [sources]. Our clients are [short description of clients].

Use only official documents and reputable legal or news publications, never blogs or marketing pages. Every item needs a working link to its source. Never invent facts, dates, numbers or links, and say so when something is unclear. Do not repeat items from earlier digests if you can see them. Leave out anything that does not matter to our clients.

Write the digest in [language] in this format, with the most important items first and no more than eight items.

Regulatory digest, [date]

[Number]. [Short headline in plain words]
What happened [one or two sentences]
Why it matters for our clients [one sentence]
Date to note [deadline or effective date, if any]
Source [name and link]

If there is nothing relevant, reply with one line. "Regulatory digest, [date]. Nothing new for your practice areas today."

End with one line. "This digest is a monitoring aid, not legal advice. Read the source before relying on any item."
```

## Good to know

Scheduling depends on your assistant and your plan. If yours can't schedule tasks, it gives you the filled-in daily prompt to run each morning.

The digest finds and summarizes. A lawyer still reads the source before relying on anything.
